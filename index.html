A screening tool, not a diagnosis

A hearing screener kids think is just a game.

Shrutimitra is a gamified audiometer — software that screens the general population for hearing issues, built mainly for children. A short set of adaptive games in place of a clinical booth, with a simple Pass or Refer result at the end.

Every child who plays a Shrutimitra game is being screened, not diagnosed — children marked "Refer" are guided to a hospital with a proper audiometer for a full clinical test.

The problem

12k+ children go without a routine hearing check every year
  • Most schools have no way to run a clinical hearing exam on site.
  • A proper audiometry test needs a trained audiologist, a sound-proof booth, and a fee most families can't justify for a check-up.
  • So hearing loss in children usually surfaces late — after it's already shaped how they learn and speak.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shrutimitra a diagnosis?+

No. Shrutimitra is a screening tool, not a diagnostic device. It flags children who may need a closer look and gives them a Pass or Refer result — a Refer means we recommend a full clinical test at a proper audiometry centre, not that hearing loss is confirmed.

How long does a screening take?+

Each game takes just a few minutes to play, so a full class can usually be screened in a single session with no appointments or waiting.

Do we need special equipment?+

Just calibrated headphones and a device to run the games on — no sound-proof booth or clinical audiometer required on site.

What happens if a child is marked "Refer"?+

They're guided through a short referral step that routes them to a nearby DEIC, PHC, or other centre for a complete clinical hearing test.

Can Shrutimitra be used at home as well as in schools?+

Yes — Shrutimitra supports both school-wide screening sessions and individual home testing.
